# Product

## Summary
Marketing + portfolio site that converts visitors into qualified leads for Dotan Meyer, a solo
waterproofing consultant and home inspector with 23+ years in the Israeli construction industry.
Its job: load instantly, rank well (classic + AI search), build trust, and drive contact.

## Who it's for
- **Primary:** Israeli homeowners and apartment buyers needing a home inspection (בדק בית) or
  waterproofing diagnosis/consultation (יועץ איטום) — often pre-purchase or with an active leak/defect.
- **Secondary:** contractors, industrial clients (floor coatings), and referrers (lawyers, realtors).

## Services
Waterproofing consultation · home inspections · industrial floor-coating specs · building-defect
reports (court-grade) · construction supervision. Service area: all of Israel.

## Value / positioning (what makes him win)
- **Rare combined expertise:** both יועץ איטום *and* בדק בית, 23+ yrs, court-grade reports.
- Real before/after project proof; named past clients (e.g. Caesarstone, Ackerstein).
- Opportunity (from competitor research): the niche is mostly stuck in ~2015 design — a modern,
  fast, mobile-first RTL site is itself a differentiator.

## v3 success criteria
- A genuinely modern, distinctive design that looks **awesome on mobile / tablet / desktop**.
- Keep/raise the technical bar: Lighthouse 93–100, LCP < 2.5s (currently 262ms), CLS ~0, zero-JS-by-default.
- Stronger conversion: surfaced trust (stats, credentials, reviews, before/after), clear CTAs.
- Drastically improved SEO + AIO/GEO (schema graph, answer-first Hebrew content, entity/E-E-A-T).
- Hebrew/RTL correct throughout. Logo preserved; palette modernized.

## Out of scope (v3)
- AI product features (chatbot, photo diagnosis) — deferred post-launch.
- WhatsApp Business integration — pending owner decision.
- Migration off Astro / off static hosting — explicitly rejected (see decisions.md).
- E-commerce, accounts, booking system, multi-language (Hebrew only for now).
